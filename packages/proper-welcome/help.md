# At home with Proper

This guide is included with your computer and works offline.

## Find an app

Click the Proper button on the taskbar to open the launcher. Type an app’s name,
or choose a favourite. **Start Here → Find your apps** opens the optional catalogue.
Select a product to read about it, then choose **Install** or **Open**.

## Make it yours

Open **Start Here → Choose your look**. Preview a desktop style, choose a text size,
or browse wallpapers. Each section has its own Apply action; selecting a preview
alone leaves your desktop unchanged. Apps and Start Here also offer **Translucent
navigation**; turn it off for a solid background.

## Files and windows

Click the folder on the taskbar to open Files. Places in the sidebar leads to your
folders, drives and network locations. Use the toolbar to go back; deleted files
normally go to Trash, where they can be restored.

Drag a title bar to move a window. The title-bar buttons minimise, maximise and
close it. Drag to a screen edge to arrange windows beside one another. Right-click
a title bar for more window actions. **Start Here → Keyboard shortcuts** lists
accelerators alongside their pointer routes.

## Sound, displays and connections

Click a status icon on the taskbar for its quick controls. Open **Start Here →
System Settings** for the complete controls, or search Settings for what you need.
Some controls appear only when the corresponding device is connected.

## Keep things up to date

Choose **Start Here → Check for updates** to open the system updater. Review its
available updates and follow its instructions; save your work before a requested
restart. Fedora supplies normal system and security updates. Proper’s own preview
releases are built from verified release source; the updater does not download a
separate Proper release automatically.

## When something goes wrong

Check the relevant connection or device in System Settings first. If an app fails
to open, try opening it from the launcher. Keep the exact error message and the
steps that caused it; these are more useful than a screenshot alone.

For a report, include the app name, what you expected, what happened, and whether
the problem repeats. Remove private information from screenshots and logs.

[Open the project and feedback on GitHub](https://github.com/marcodenic/PROPER_LINUX)
(requires internet and access to the repository). If that page is unavailable,
share the details with the person who supplied your Proper preview. This local
guide remains available without a GitHub account.
